Colour is an early-stage materials foundry. We design, make and test thermal and magnetic materials in our own lab, built around one measure: the shortest path from a customer's specification to an independently tested sample.

## 01 / The shortage

Hardware is hitting two limits at once, heat and supply. Both are materials problems.

- Demand for magnet rare earths has **doubled since 2015** (International Energy Agency).
- **Seven heavy rare earths** have been under Chinese export controls since April 2025 (International Energy Agency).
- **Over half** of electronic device failures are linked to heat, according to thermal materials maker Element Six.

## 02 / The edge

**One lab. One loop.**

Materials work is usually split across suppliers, labs and consultants, and every handoff costs time. We keep the whole path from specification to verified sample under one roof, record every run, and make each project sharpen the next one.

We measure days from signed spec to released datasheet on every project, and we will publish the numbers, good or bad. The details of our process stay in the lab. The proof is published.

## 04 / Our story

**Materials should be engineered, not just extracted.**

For centuries, civilisation has taken its materials from the ground and accepted the limits that came with it. We think the next era belongs to materials designed with precision, made in controlled laboratories, recycled where possible and measured at every step.

Atoms do not come from nothing, so we are plain about our aim: reduce dependence on virgin extraction, one verified material at a time. Prove one material. Partner with customers. Scale the foundry.
