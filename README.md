Colour is an early-stage materials research company. We design, make and independently verify engineered materials, starting with copper-diamond thermal composites for power electronics and high-temperature magnets that use less heavy rare earth.

## Programmes

| Programme | The problem | Hypothesis | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Thermal composites** | Heat spreading and thermal-expansion mismatch in power electronics | Control the copper-diamond interface and tune the diamond fraction to balance conductivity and expansion | Research planning. No samples. |
| **High-temperature magnets** | Magnets weaken in hot motors and depend on scarce heavy rare earths | Combine grain boundary diffusion with hydrogen processing of scrap, subject to patent review | Literature stage. No samples. |

## How we label evidence

| Label | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Established** | Standard textbook science or widely replicated results |
| **Reported** | A named source's result that we have not reproduced |
| **Hypothesis** | A reasoned proposal we intend to test |
| **Unknown** | No evidence found either way |

We have a commercial interest in these materials, and we disclose it. Our policies are in the [research repository](https://github.com/thecolourfoundation/colour-materials-research).

## Work with us

- **Engineering teams:** send a thermal or magnet specification to buildwithcolours@gmail.com. We scope a study or sample evaluation, with a written quote before any work.
- **Researchers and reviewers:** challenge our claims by opening an [issue](https://github.com/thecolourfoundation/colour-materials-research/issues). Corrections are credited.
- **Pioneers:** we are looking for founding engineers, scientists and technical advisors. [See the roles](https://thecolourfoundation.github.io/colour-website/pioneers.html).

## Principles

Proof before promise. Your specification stays yours. Honest data, including the misses.
